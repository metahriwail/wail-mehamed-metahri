✳ How did we get here?

We believe in the power of pointlessness.

Other museums collect treasures. We collect questionable decisions, charming accidents, and ideas that nobody asked for.

Our mission is simple: remind the world that creativity doesn't always need a purpose. Sometimes it just needs a spoon with holes.

Sincerely,

The Curator of Nonsense
